## Connecting computers

The overview shows which computer supplies the keyboard and mouse, the receiving computer,
and the current connection status. Choose a role before starting a new connection.

On the computer with your keyboard and mouse, choose **Use this computer’s keyboard and mouse**.
Use **Arrange** to configure the receiving computers, then choose **Start sharing**.
On the receiving computer, choose **Use the other computer’s keyboard and mouse** and enter the
controlling computer’s name or IP address. Previously used addresses are available through
**Saved computers**. TLS fingerprint confirmation follows the existing security settings.

Use **Connection** to open the detailed connection controls, **Settings** for application
preferences, and **Diagnostics** to open the connection controls and log. Use **Back to overview**
to return. The overview scrolls in smaller windows and moves navigation below the content
when the sidebar cannot fit.
